Why these 3 first

They cover all three check methods: a scan (AIVM-2), an API attestation (IAM 2) and an AI-assisted control (DP-3), so the whole framework is proven early.

Why AI starts in September

AI controls need about three months in advisory mode to measure accuracy before they can block a release.

Application rollout

2–3 pilot apps in Q2, wider onboarding with each wave, all applications by December 2027.
